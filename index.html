🛡️ Severe Weather Emergency Guide

🌀 Cyclone & Gale Wind Protocols

  • Remain strictly indoors in a reinforced central room away from windows and glass doors.
  • Anchor or store inside all loose patio furniture, signs, and roofing tiles.
  • Keep flashlights, first-aid kits, backup power banks, and battery radios fully charged.
  • Shut off main power circuit breakers and LPG gas cylinders in case of storm damage.
  • Never step outside when the calm "eye" of the storm passes over; violent reverse winds follow immediately.

🌊 Heavy Rain & Flash Flood Protocols

  • Never attempt to drive or walk through flooded roadways or submerged underpasses.
  • Turn around, don't drown: 30 cm (12 in) of rushing floodwater can float and sweep away most vehicles.
  • Elevate electrical appliances and critical documents to higher floor levels.
  • Steer clear of damaged electrical transmission poles and downed cables to avoid electrocution.
  • Boil or purify all tap water before drinking following severe flooding.

🔥 Extreme Heatwave & Thermal Safety Protocols

  • Drink ample amounts of water and electrolyte-rich liquids continuously throughout the day.
  • Refrain from strenuous outdoor physical activities between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM.
  • Dress in lightweight, light-colored, loose cotton garments with broad-brimmed hats and sunglasses.
  • Never leave infants, elderly, or pets inside unattended parked cars for any period.
  • Emergency Heatstroke Symptoms: high body temperature, red/dry skin, nausea, confusion, or fainting. Call emergency medical aid immediately.
